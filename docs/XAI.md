# Finpluse Explainable AI

## Philosophy

Every AI decision must be auditable and understandable by the user.
No "black box" outputs.

## Components

1. **Reasoning Traces**: Step-by-step explanation of how each decision was made
2. **SHAP Integration**: Feature attribution for anomaly and forecast decisions
3. **Counterfactuals**: "If you reduce dining by $100, runway extends 12 days"
4. **Mathematical Transparency**: LaTeX formulas for deterministic calculations
5. **Audit Log**: Immutable record of every AI decision with user feedback
